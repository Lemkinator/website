Want to transform long and cumbersome URLs into short, elegant links? OneURL is your answer!
OneURL is a powerful URL shortener and QR code generator that will revolutionize your online experience.

## Features

### 🔗 Effortless URL Shortening

Turn long web addresses into short, user-friendly links. OneURL supports multiple URL shortening services.

### 🌐 Custom alias

Personalize your shortened URLs to your heart's content! Set custom alias to make your links even more personalized.

### ✏️ Add Descriptions

Never forget what your links are about. Add useful descriptions to keep track of the content of each link.

### 🌟 QR Codes

OneURL automatically generates QR codes for your shortened links. Additionally, OneURL provides an
integrated QR code generator. With this handy tool, you can effortlessly create QR codes for any links.
Share information even faster and more conveniently by simply having the generated QR code scanned.

### 📲 Direct Sharing

Share your shortened links and QR codes directly via social media, email, messenger apps, and more.
Effortlessly share whenever you desire.

### ✂️ Copy and Paste

Copy shortened links and QR codes with just one click and paste them into messages, emails, or documents.
