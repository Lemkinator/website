## Devices

The app is available in the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=de.lemke.nakbuch)
and can be used on all Android smartphones, tablets, [smartwatches](#wear-os-support) and
[Windows computers](#windows-support). A Lite version of NAKBuch was developed as a
[web app](#web-app-nakbuch-lite) for other devices.

## Features

- Free
- Dark Mode (automatically adapts to the system settings)
- Quick setting for mute/do not disturb mode
- Pleasant text view
- Time signature, key signature and starting pitches
- Favorites
- Ratings
- Notes
- Date function (sung on...)
- History

## No support for Apple devices

NAKBuch is not available for Apple devices for various reasons.

For one, Apple requires developers to pay an annual fee of 99 USD/299 USD for the "Apple Developer
Program" / "Apple Developer Enterprise Program" and, on the other hand, it is not possible with my
development setup to create apps for iOS or macOS, as Apple prescribes the use of macOS and the
integrated development environment Xcode for the creation of iOS and macOS apps, regardless of technical
requirements.

In addition, any processes to publish an app in the App Store are very time-consuming and destroy the
entire motivation to develop a free app for Apple devices.

As long as Apple does not change anything about these peculiar conditions, there will be no app for iOS
or macOS.

## Web App (NAKBuch Lite)

To make the app available for other devices as well, a web app was developed. It offers the most
important functions of the Android app and can be used on all devices with a modern browser. NAKBuch
Lite was developed as a Progressive Web App (PWA), can be added to the homescreen and behaves almost
like a native app.

NAKBuch Lite: [www.nakbuch.de](https://www.nakbuch.de)
